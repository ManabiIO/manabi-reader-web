# Get started

You can start without an account.

1. Open [Manabi Reader for Web](https://manabi.io/reader-web/).
2. Open **Library actions** (the ellipsis), then **Add Books**.
3. Choose **Import File(s)** or **Import Folder(s)**. Select an EPUB, HTMLZ, or text file, or a folder of books.
4. Select a cover to read.

On supported browsers, you can also connect a local folder from **Accounts and Libraries**. The reader can then show the books in that folder without importing copies. Your browser may ask you to grant access again later.

To bring reading data from another installation, see [Accounts and data](accounts-and-data.md). If you used Ttsu, see [Move from Ttsu](move-from-ttsu.md).
