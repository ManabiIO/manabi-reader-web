# Move from Ttsu

You can bring a Ttsu Ebook Reader export into Manabitan Reader.

1. Export your data from Ttsu.
2. In Manabitan Reader, open **Library actions** → **Add Books** → **Import from Ttu Ebook Reader**.
3. Choose the export, review the preview, and select what to import.

The importer can bring parsed books, bookmarks, reading statistics, and supported settings. Keep the original export as a backup. Imported data does not create an ongoing connection to Ttsu or its storage account.

For the feature differences, see [Manabitan Reader versus Ttsu Reader](compare/ttsu.md).
